\begin{abstract}
Stopping a reasoning model requires information available before its unseen
continuation, whereas full-trajectory classification can exploit later answers.
We study complete response-and-revision increments and separate immediate
correctness drift from optimal continuation value. A finite-horizon formulation
specializes the Snell envelope to correctness minus charged computation; a drift
crossing is optimal under persistent conditional signs, but two exact
counterexamples invalidate weaker population or myopic arguments. We audit a
75,965-trajectory revision matrix and a separate 28,888-trajectory five-step
detector corpus. A retrospective stacked AUC of 0.955156 uses future information
and non-nested fitting; task-grouped causal-prefix GRU AUC is 0.874326 in a
different evaluation. We implement a causal runtime and train portable current
and next-correctness predictors using disjoint task groups. On 100 actual
Qwen2.5-0.5B generations, the frozen learned policy saves 56.51\% of completion
tokens, with seven correct answers versus six for the full horizon. Every task
stops at two responses, and the accuracy interval includes harm. Thus actual
avoided generation is established, while added value over fixed-two stopping,
live calibration, and accuracy noninferiority are not.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Additional reasoning can repair an answer, repeat an unproductive calculation,
or replace a correct candidate with an incorrect revision. Final-answer accuracy
conceals these transitions. A stopping system must decide after an observed
prefix whether a further increment has enough expected benefit to justify its
cost. This is a conditional allocation problem, not a claim that longer
reasoning is invariably helpful or harmful. It also differs from identifying
the best step after the complete trajectory has been graded.

We investigate repeated \emph{complete responses}: a model emits a thought,
candidate answer, and telemetry, and the next prompt includes its earlier
responses. An increment is consequently a response-and-revision event rather
than an arbitrary token or an observation of hidden cognition. This contract
makes both the decision information and the avoided model calls inspectable.
Its conclusions need not transfer to uninterrupted internal reasoning.

Three distinctions motivate the study. First, corruption, insufficient
cost-adjusted benefit, and regret against a comparator are different endpoints.
Accuracy can increase while net utility drift is negative. Second, immediate
drift omits the option of future repair, so its sign need not determine an
optimal stop. Third, ranking, probability calibration, and stopping utility
are different statistical targets. A high full-trajectory AUC cannot be
inherited by a controller that cannot see those future observations.

Our contribution is an integrated theoretical and empirical audit rather than
a claim to invent the Snell envelope. We state the admissible stopping problem,
prove the specialized reward reduction and conditional transition identity,
give a sufficient persistent-sign condition, and exhibit exact failures of
weaker arguments. We then bind two overlapping experimental corpora to
explicit denominators, retain matched negative results, and distinguish an
inadmissible retrospective diagnostic from causal-prefix evaluation. Finally,
we execute an observation-only controller with a serialized trained predictor,
separate fitting from calibration and evaluation, and report actual generation
costs alongside low absolute task accuracy. The learned rule's near collapse to
a fixed-two baseline is itself an important negative finding.

\paragraph{Related work.}
Chain-of-thought prompting demonstrates the utility of explicit intermediate
reasoning \citep{wei2022}, while self-consistency aggregates sampled reasoning
paths \citep{wang2022}. Outcome verification and process supervision address
different forms of answer assessment \citep{cobbe2021,lightman2023}; neither
alone defines an admissible stopping time with all assessment costs charged.
Work on overthinking in long-reasoning models motivates economical
inference \citep{chen2024}. Our observational unit and controlled revision
prompt are narrower, and we do not treat our counts as measurements of those
models' latent thoughts. The benchmarks are GSM8K, MATH, ARC-Challenge and
GPQA \citep{cobbe2021,hendrycks2021,clark2018,rein2023}. GRU and rotary-position
architectures supply reference sequence components \citep{cho2014,su2021}.
Classical finite-horizon stopping theory supplies the general optimum
\citep{ferguson,peskir2006}; concentration and confidence-sequence theory
clarify the additional assumptions needed for uncertainty certificates
\citep{hoeffding1963,howard2021}. We use these results to delimit empirical
claims, rather than equating a fitted score with their conditional objects.

\section{Decision model and optimality limits}
\label{sec:theory}
Fix a finite horizon \(N\) and earliest permitted stop \(m\). On a probability
space, let \(\F_t\) be the growing declared decision information after response
\(t\). A causal selector emits \(A_t\); \(C_t=\ind\{A_t=Y^*\}\) denotes its
correctness against the reference, with the benchmark grader replacing exact
equality where appropriate. Cumulative computation cost \(K_t\) is nonnegative,
nondecreasing, adapted and integrable. All executed probes and peers enter both
the information and cost ledgers. Offline gold and future responses are not
runtime inputs. An admissible stopping time satisfies
\(m\le\tau\le N\) and \(\{\tau\le t\}\in\F_t\).

This is a statistical information model, not a formalization of computational
hardness. If the reference is a known measurable deterministic function of a
fully revealed task, then \(C_t\) is measurable and its conditional probability
equals its label, regardless of a gold API field. Nondegenerate uncertainty
requires a latent-reference model or an explicitly coarser decision-statistic
filtration. A fitted posterior over prefix summaries does not condition on all
mathematical semantics. No such sufficiency is inferred from its AUC.

Put \(q_t=\E[C_t\mid\F_t]\) and \(G_t=q_t-K_t\). For every admissible \(\tau\),
\begin{equation}
 \E[C_\tau-K_\tau]=\E[G_\tau].
 \label{eq:reduction}
\end{equation}
Indeed, \(\{\tau=t\}\) is measurable in \(\F_t\), so multiplying \(C_t\) and
\(q_t\) by its indicator preserves expectation; sum over the bounded times.
The candidate selector is material. Our learned and full-horizon arms select
the latest nonempty available answer, so a blank current response can carry
an earlier answer forward. The heuristic adds causal retention branches
specified below.

\paragraph{Conditional transitions.}
Define repair and corruption masses
\(r_t=\PP(C_t=0,C_{t+1}=1\mid\F_t)\) and
\(s_t=\PP(C_t=1,C_{t+1}=0\mid\F_t)\).
Set \(\alpha_t=r_t/(1-q_t)\) when \(q_t<1\) and
\(\beta_t=s_t/q_t\) when \(q_t>0\), assigning zero on null states. With
\(c_t=\E[K_{t+1}-K_t\mid\F_t]\),
\begin{equation}
 \mu_t:=\E[G_{t+1}-G_t\mid\F_t]
       =(1-q_t)\alpha_t-q_t\beta_t-c_t .
 \label{eq:drift}
\end{equation}
The binary increment is a repair indicator minus a corruption indicator, and
the tower property gives the identity. It requires neither Markov structure
nor independent transitions. The hazards have different eligible denominators;
their unweighted difference is not accuracy drift. For correct-answer value
\(v\) and incorrect-answer penalty \(p\), the reward is
\((v+p)q_t-p-K_t\), and the accuracy part of the drift is multiplied by \(v+p\).

\begin{theorem}[Finite-horizon optimum]
\label{thm:snell}
Under the stated integrability, adaptation and bounded stopping assumptions,
define \(S_N=G_N\) and
\begin{equation}
 S_t=\max\{G_t,\E[S_{t+1}\mid\F_t]\},\quad m\le t<N.
 \label{eq:snell}
\end{equation}
Then \(S\) is the smallest integrable supermartingale dominating \(G\), and
\(\tau_t^*=\inf\{s\ge t:S_s=G_s\}\) attains
\(S_t=\esssup_{\tau\ge t}\E[G_\tau\mid\F_t]\).
\end{theorem}
\noindent
Backward induction establishes domination and minimality. Bounded optional
sampling upper-bounds every admissible reward by \(S_t\). Before first contact,
the recursion holds with continuation equality; the stopped process is a
martingale from \(t\) and attains the bound at contact. Appendix
\ref{app:proofs} gives complete proofs. This is the standard finite-horizon
construction \citep{ferguson,peskir2006}, specialized to the declared selector
and floor.

Optimal continuation advantage includes an additional option:
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_t^*=\E[S_{t+1}\mid\F_t]-G_t
 =\mu_t+\E[S_{t+1}-G_{t+1}\mid\F_t]\ge\mu_t.
 \label{eq:option}
\end{equation}
Thus positive immediate drift favors continuation, but nonpositive drift alone
does not establish an optimal stop.

\begin{proposition}[Persistent-sign sufficient condition]
\label{prop:persistence}
Let \(T_c\) be the first admissible \(t<N\) with \(\mu_t\le0\), or \(N\).
If, almost surely, every later conditional drift stays nonpositive, then
\(T_c\) maximizes expected reward over admissible stopping times.
\end{proposition}
\noindent
For any bounded \(\tau\), the drift decomposition gives
\(\E G_\tau=\E G_m+\E\sum_{s=m}^{N-1}\ind\{\tau>s\}\mu_s\).
Before \(T_c\), stopping earlier omits positive terms; afterward, continuing
adds nonpositive terms. This proves the comparison. Pathwise increasing \(q\),
decreasing \(\alpha\), increasing \(\beta\), and nondecreasing predictable cost
are sufficient for nonincreasing drift, but are not established for the model
panels. A single crossing of a population curve is weaker.

\paragraph{Two exact counterexamples.}
With \(q=(0,0,1)\) and cost \(0.1\) per increment, immediate drift at zero is
\(-0.1\); myopic stopping earns zero, while stopping at two earns \(0.8\).
The example shifts to any floor. A second construction starts at \(q_0=0.5\),
observes a fair branch with \(q_1=0.4\) or \(0.6\), then has \(q_2=1\) or \(0\)
respectively. Both unconditional drifts are \(-0.1\), yet continuing once and
then continuing only on the first branch earns \(0.65>0.5\). Appendix
\ref{app:counterexamples} realizes both with one common reference answer;
neither uses a hindsight decision.

\paragraph{Estimation and uncertainty.}
A feature-conditional predictor need not equal \(q_t\) or provide a sufficient
state for a compressed Bellman recursion. Calibration is a separate prediction
objective \citep{guo2017}; marginal calibration need not imply history-conditional
calibration (Appendix \ref{app:calibration}). Weighted binary log loss
has optimum \(r=w_1q/[w_1q+w_0(1-q)]\), requiring correction or independent
calibration before probability interpretation. If simultaneous upper bounds
cover \(\mu_t\), stopping on a nonpositive bound controls false positive-drift
stops at the coverage error; it does not prove Bellman optimality or
noninferiority. Probe independence must be stated given pre-probe information
with a named target. Post-probe drift can differ. Conditional supermartingale
assumptions likewise cannot be replaced by favorable unconditional means
\citep{hoeffding1963,howard2021}. We do not claim such live certificates.

\section{Corpora, audit protocol, and trained controller}
\label{sec:methods}
\paragraph{Two overlapping records.}
The revision matrix contains 52 model-domain cells, 13 model configurations,
75,965 sanitized trajectories, 798,770 raw step rows and 1,948 unique tasks.
Recorded horizons are 8 for ARC, 10 for GSM8K/GPQA and 14 for MATH. The
standardized detector selection contains 52 cells, 13 configurations,
28,888 five-step trajectories, 144,440 rows and 2,948 tasks; it replaces
InternLM with Qwen3.5-9B. They overlap and must not be summed. Historical
generation uses seed 7 at temperatures 0.1, 0.6 and 1.0, not independent
generation seeds. GPQA metadata and loader split provenance differ; the
selected record has no SVAMP result. These details limit generalization.

The freeze distinguishes exact local bytes and canonical LF content to handle
checkout line endings. Hashes bind selected records, not their truth,
representativeness or every grader outcome. Primary archive analyses retain
stored labels; new training labels are a separately audited version. Task
resampling preserves repeated trajectories, while matched estimator
contrasts use their declared 52-cell uncertainty unit. Historical environment
metadata are incomplete. We provide verifiable saved-result reanalysis rather
than claim bitwise reexecution on arbitrary hardware.

\paragraph{Causal runtime.}
The controller accepts one new completed observation, stores its own prefix,
rejects skipped/repeated steps, and closes after stopping. It cannot receive
gold, correctness, a future row or a full-sequence score. The live generator
requests one response at a time and removes stopped tasks from later calls.
Confidence is trusted as \emph{observed telemetry} only after strict validation
of the required JSON fields; fallback defaults become missing confidence.
Reported confidence is not a calibrated posterior. Optional peers require a
complete same-step roster with a barrier preceding the decision and fully
charged costs; these experiments use no peers or verifier.

Both active and full-horizon arms use floor two, horizon five and the same
response boundary. Learned and baseline selection use the latest nonempty
answer. The heuristic freezes confidence 90, stability two, confidence drop
15 and two changes as its wobble setting; its drop branch retains the previous
answer and its wobble branch retains the highest observed valid confidence.
Neither retention branch fires in the reported live panels, so realized
selection uses the common default.
The learned rule instead stops on
\(\widehat p_{\mathrm{next}}-\widehat q_t-0.05\le0\) after the floor. The cost is
a declared utility preference, not a universal monetary rate. At the horizon
no next-step head is queried.

\paragraph{Prefix probability fitting.}
We train on all 1,500 archived Qwen2.5-0.5B GSM8K-training and MATH trajectories
\citep{qwen2024}. A fixed salted hash of public domain/task identity assigns
902 tasks to training, 322 to calibration and 276 to evaluation before parser
availability or outcomes. Public identities of the live 100 tasks and 20 traps
are excluded. Each task's rows remain in one role. Two unweighted logistic
heads target correctness of the latest nonempty candidate currently selected
and of the candidate selected by the same rule after one more increment.
Train-only standardization and logistic coefficients use \(C=1\),
L-BFGS and 2,000 maximum iterations. Independent one-dimensional logistic
calibrators use only calibration tasks, \(C=1000\) and the same iteration cap.
No evaluation/live/trap label selects a feature, cost or threshold.

The 21-feature contract uses current/prior step, parse success, answer presence,
valid confidence and prefix summaries, generated-token lengths, answer changes
and streak, answer/thought characters, fixed thought numeric and keyword
densities, public domain and predetermined interactions. It excludes hidden
states, labels, future responses and retrospective normalization. Runtime
requires only the standard library: ordered coefficients, scalers and
calibrators are serialized with source and task-split identities.

Reconstructing archived candidates through the actual live JSON/fallback parser
produces 4,269 string disagreements among 7,500 rows. We regrade against
\emph{archived} references under the existing verifier; 43 correctness labels
change. A row-level audit preserves both versions and the original corpus.
Requiring all five candidates to agree would select an early prefix using
later observations, so that provisional eligibility approach was rejected.
All complete trajectories are retained. None is strict-valid live JSON,
leaving confidence-related features without archive variation; the live prompt
and emitted-EOS accounting are material transport changes.

\paragraph{Causality, transport and artifact validation.}
The split protects three distinct statistical operations: coefficient fitting,
probability calibration, and final development measurement. A standardizer
estimated on evaluation prefixes would breach that boundary even without
reading their labels; a trajectory-agreement eligibility filter would breach
it by reading unavailable future candidates. Our selected-row and
serialization checks address these concrete failure modes. Current/next targets
also refer to selected answers, not merely the most recently emitted string.
Ignoring carry-forward selection creates a different supervised target precisely
when a response is empty or cannot be parsed.

These protections do not establish transport to the JSON live prompt.
The seven parse/confidence-related feature coefficients are zero in both
heads because their archived inputs are constant. The deployed rule is therefore
a learned function of the other supported prefix statistics, not evidence that
self-reported confidence has been calibrated. Independently calibrated
logistic heads can also have different approximation errors; subtracting their
probabilities does not guarantee an unbiased drift or a uniformly covered
conditional gain. We retain both head diagnostics and evaluate their actual
stopping actions instead of calling the probability difference a certified
hazard. No monotonicity constraint or Bellman transition model is fitted.

Runtime validation rejects wrong feature order, nonfinite coefficients,
nonpositive scaler denominators and unsupported horizons/domains. Standard-library
predictions are checked against the offline implementation. These are useful
deployment consistency checks, but they verify execution of the frozen model,
not its statistical correctness. The anonymous review supplement contains
source/evidence documentation and sanitized review copies with original/review
identities. It omits large raw archives and model weights, so it supports
focused audit rather than complete historical rerunning.

\paragraph{Verification boundary and AI assistance.}
LLM coding agents contributed mathematical drafting, source audits, implementation,
label-reconstruction design, and manuscript preparation. Verification includes
independent finite-policy enumeration, numeric serialization checks,
task-partition and prefix-invariance tests, artifact hashes and primary-source
reference checks. Agents are not authors, and these checks are not a completed
human author or independent reviewer attestation. Human verification and
release/license review remain pending for this preparation draft.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
\subsection{Population transitions and controlled contrasts}
At the pooled GSM8K step-two prefix, 3,145 repairs occur among 14,818 incorrect
candidates and 1,170 corruptions among 4,682 correct candidates. On the common
19,500-trajectory, 500-task panel, their difference divided by trajectory count
and minus cost 0.05 is \(+0.0513\). At prefix four, accuracy still increases by
approximately \(0.0373\), yet net drift is \(-0.0127\). Consequently a negative
utility drift is not necessarily an accuracy decline.

Selected cells also contradict a universal early crossing. Qwen2.5-7B/GSM8K
has drift \(+0.01933\) at prefix four and \(-0.02533\) at five; 32B/MATH has
\(+0.01867\) at five and \(-0.01333\) at six. Each cell has 1,500 trajectories
over 500 tasks. These are descriptive conditional-on-panel summaries, not
per-question posterior bounds or proof of persistent conditional signs.

elsewhere.

\subsection{Retrospective ranking and fitted prefix probabilities}
The stored task-grouped causal GRU has micro AUC 0.874326, task-macro 0.821392,
domain-macro 0.810242 and worst-domain GPQA AUC 0.631253. Task AUC requires both
classes; 2,679 of 2,948 groups are eligible. A causal RoPE transformer has
lower micro AUC 0.863837 but slightly higher recorded step utility
0.333123 versus 0.326449. Their descriptive fold intervals overlap;
no statistically unique architecture winner follows.

The retrospective stacked score is 0.955156 versus 0.943223 for its
reduced-feature LightGBM control, with lift interval [0.010384,0.013480].
The interval concerns the difference, not the absolute stacked AUC.
Bidirectional all-five-step scoring and centered smoothing consume future
observations, while meta-training is not nested within reported scoring outer
folds. The nominal \emph{No Peers} control retains committee aggregates. Hence the
stored lift isolates neither live correctness nor a pure peer effect.
Bootstrap repetition does not repair information leakage. Moreover the
0.955156 and 0.874326 numbers are different evaluations; subtracting them cannot
identify a causal effect of removing future features.

\subsection{Actual generation, costs and latency}
We execute local Qwen2.5-0.5B with greedy CUDA generation, batch size 32,
128-token completion cap, floor two and horizon five. A cached GSM8K-test
100-task panel is fixed before collection; a separate 20-question handpicked
mathematical bank covers rates, changing bases, dependent draws and answer
contracts. Public prompts are separated from grading ledgers. The learned
artifact is frozen before its active runs and paired with the actually executed
prior full-horizon baseline linked by hashes. Reusing that baseline avoids
redundant GPU work without converting active execution into saved-prefix replay.
Shared-prefix agreement is nevertheless measured, not presumed from greedy
decoding.

This uses simultaneous 97.5\% Clopper--Pearson intervals \citep{clopper1934} for improvement and

[45.86,57.17]\%; accuracy-change bounds span [-19.68,+19.68] points.
For that handpicked bank the iid-reference bound and resampling are
descriptive, not randomized adversarial-population coverage. No accuracy
noninferiority claim follows.

All 100 learned main tasks and 20 traps terminate at two, so the result cannot
establish an advantage over fixed two. The original heuristic's modest 2.94\%
main saving is far below a 30--40\% target. Baseline strict JSON success is only
88/500 responses; confidence guarding largely forces its mean stop to 4.85.
The learned rule ignores full-trajectory scores and improves compute avoidance,
but its low absolute accuracy and absent archive JSON support constrain its
statistical interpretation.

The observed action equivalence is particularly informative. On these runs,
every permitted first decision terminates the task; none uses later prefixes
to choose between repair and corruption. A deterministic two-response budget
would choose the same stopping indices on the realized histories. The model's
held-out AUC therefore cannot be interpreted as evidence of useful adaptive
allocation on this live panel. A separate independently executed fixed-two
arm is not reported, and the historical one-trace exception at response three
does not overcome this limitation. The comparison with never-stop still
measures avoided actual generation under a declared common response contract.

Only one main task changes correctness between learned and full-horizon arms.
The exact paired interval accordingly includes accuracy loss despite a
positive point estimate, and the trap panel is both smaller and purposively
selected. More repeated timing measurements cannot narrow this task-level
accuracy uncertainty or make the bank representative. Prespecifying a tolerance
and powering a future study for that tolerance would change the evidentiary
question; inspecting these results cannot retroactively register a margin.
Similarly, unchanged aggregate accuracy for the original heuristic is not
proof of accuracy preservation on a new task population.

Prompt and physical work differ from emitted tokens. Main baseline versus
learned totals are 142,498 versus 43,874 input tokens, 57,856 versus 23,564
decode slots, and 1,127.05 versus 343.25 model seconds. These observed ledger
quantities are not interchangeable with energy or universal speedup. Peers and
verifier generation are zero. A final saved-MATH prepared-observation benchmark
has 18,880 combined heuristic/control decisions over 100 problems and 20
repeats, maximum 0.4383 ms. The separate learned-main benchmark has 4,000
decisions and maximum 0.9369 ms, including both calibrated heads. These exclude
model loading, generation, tokenization and peer waits, and are worst observed
values rather than worst-case timing proofs.

\section{Limitations, impact, and conclusion}
\label{sec:limits}
The study establishes a causal interface and measured development trade-offs,
not universal economical reasoning. Its theoretical optimality is conditional
on a specified process, filtration, selector and reward; persistent conditional
signs and feature-state sufficiency are unverified empirically. The live model
is small, responses often fail the structured contract, prompts differ from
training, and the frozen learned policy reduces to fixed two. Task groups
protect fitting boundaries but do not establish untouched external development
history, representative adversarial coverage or multi-seed generalization.
The historical detector's upstream evaluation dependence is retained as a
limitation, not repaired by downstream bootstrap statistics.

Avoiding unnecessary calls can reduce inference work, but a controller can
also terminate before repair or hide failures behind confident stable answers.
Low accuracy and subgroup variation make a cost-only deployment claim
inappropriate. No energy benefit is measured. A stronger confirmatory study
needs unseen tasks, prespecified accuracy tolerance, several generation seeds,
a frozen transported model, subgroup analysis and all prompt/probe costs.
Human verification and an anonymous licensed artifact release remain pending.
An anonymous review capsule is prepared, but complete public release and
asset-license review are different steps. Before a confirmatory run, the
decision information, candidate selector, cost units, model/parser versions,
data roles, minimum budget and tie convention should be frozen together.
Changing a prompt to improve JSON validity changes the observation law and
requires new transport validation, rather than reuse of the present calibration
claim. Subgroup auditing should distinguish benchmark, task difficulty and
parser failures while keeping all held-out tasks in the declared denominator.
Any higher-stakes application must evaluate the consequences of missed repair,
not infer suitability from economical generation alone.
The framework makes those requirements testable and exposes where attractive
retrospective numbers cease to justify an online decision.
