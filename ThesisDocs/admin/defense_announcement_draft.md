Additional reasoning can repair an incorrect answer, replace a correct answer, or consume computation without sufficient improvement. This thesis formulates response-level stopping using observable reasoning prefixes, proves the finite-horizon Bellman/Snell solution and identifies sufficient conditions and counterexamples for a simpler drift rule. Its audited experiments separate two archived corpora from actual live generation. A learned prefix policy reduces completion-token use on small GSM8K and arithmetic-trap panels, but every learned task stops at response two. Low absolute accuracy and paired uncertainty prevent claims of adaptive advantage or guaranteed accuracy preservation. The defense will discuss the mathematical assumptions, reproducible evidence, online controller and limits of the measured results.
