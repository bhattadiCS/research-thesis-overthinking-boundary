## Approval route

The [ACM guidelines revised July 13, 2026](https://ep.jhu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/EP-ACM-Research-or-Thesis-Option_guidelines.pdf), pages 3–5, require an electronic library thesis, a public defense attended by the research adviser and second reader, and adviser contact at least biweekly. The usual defense is approximately 30 minutes presenting and 30 minutes answering questions. After defense/final-thesis completion, the adviser and reader certify completion and forward the final thesis to the academic adviser, Program Manager Beryl Castello and Program Chair James Spall. Chair approval precedes the adviser's final course grade. Recheck names and requirements before actual transmission.

The [Semester 2 approval form](https://ep.jhu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/ACM-Semester-2-Thesis-Approval-Form.pdf) is a status/registration document at the end of semester 1. Its order is student, research adviser, second reader, academic adviser and chair, followed by Registrar submission. Verify the existing filing before preparing a duplicate. It is not a post-defense completion certificate.

The [625.804 webpage](https://ep.jhu.edu/courses/625804-applied-and-computational-mathematics-masters-thesis/) directs the electronic thesis to Sheridan standards and still mentions a bound copy. The newer guidelines describe electronic submission without that bound-copy requirement. Program clarification is required; no binding purchase has been made.
