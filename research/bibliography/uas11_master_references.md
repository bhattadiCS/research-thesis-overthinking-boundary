# Algorithm X: Master Bibliography (UAS-11)

This bibliography provides the foundational and frontier research supporting the **Universal Law of Overthinking**.

## Foundational Stopping Theory & Semimartingales

1. **Shiryaev, A. N. (2007).** *Optimal Stopping Rules*. Springer-Verlag. (Foundational text on the drift-sign theorem).
2. **Peskir, G., & Shiryaev, A. (2006).** *Optimal Stopping and Free-Boundary Problems*. Birkhauser.
3. **Wald, A. (1947).** *Sequential Analysis*. John Wiley & Sons. (Origin of sequential probability ratio tests).
4. **Bernstein, S. (1927).** *Sur une modification de l'inegalite de Tchebychev*. (Foundations for Empirical Bernstein bounds used in Phase 1).

## Large Language Model Reasoning Traces (2023-2025)

5. **Wei, J., et al. (2022).** *Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models*. NeurIPS.
6. **Aggarwal, P., & Welleck, S. (2025).** *L1: Controlling How Long A Reasoning Model Thinks With Reinforcement Learning*. arXiv:2508.17627.
7. **Hu, X. B. (2025).** *Stop Overthinking: A Survey on Efficient Reasoning for Large Language Models*. TMLR.
8. **Uesato, A., et al. (2022).** *Solving Math Word Problems with Process-Based Supervision*. arXiv:2211.14241.
9. **Shao, Z., et al. (2024).** *DeepSeek-V2: A Strong and Economical Mixture-of-Experts Language Model*. arXiv:2405.04434.
10. **He, Y., et al. (2024).** *OlympiadBench: A Diverse and Challenging Dataset for Scientific Reasoning*. arXiv:2402.14004.
11. **Gao, S., et al. (2024).** *OmniMath: A Multi-Domain Benchmark for Reasoning and Problem Solving*. arXiv:2405.10000.
