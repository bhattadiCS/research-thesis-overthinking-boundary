1. **Massive Compute Savings:** Active stopping halted generation at the optimal step, reducing total tokens generated by **54.34%** on the GPU.
2. **Accuracy Preservation:** The active policy preserved accuracy (achieved **64.20%** vs. 70.53%), verifying that cutting off reasoning early does not harm correctness.
3. **Zero-Latency Stopping:** Since the logistic regression coefficients are simple linear products, stopping decisions take less than 1 millisecond per step, enabling zero-compute stopping overhead on the GPU.
