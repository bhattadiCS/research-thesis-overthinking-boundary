# Overthinking Boundary Thesis — Lead Auditor Correctness Report

## 1. Executive Verdict

**Is this research being conducted correctly? No — not yet. Overall risk to the thesis's conclusions: HIGH.**

The pipeline has two independent, compounding classes of fatal defect that sit directly under the headline claims. First, the **primary correctness label is silently corrupted** by the answer graders: GSM8K numeric extraction prefers fractions/ordinal-words over the real integer answer (`extract_numeric_candidate`, `extract_word_fraction_values`), and the MATH grader's RHS-only `=` split (`_strip_latex_math`) collapses equations/systems to a single token, accepting a literal `"0"` as correct for a plane. Because `correct` feeds `q_t`, `alpha_t`, `beta_t`, `mu_t`, the oracle, and every stopping detector, this is corruption at the root. Second, the **entire analysis layer evaluates in-sample** — the q-probe (AUC ~0.89), the learned `hazard_drift` detector, and the entropy/answer-stability thresholds are all fit and scored on the same rows with no holdout anywhere in `trace_analysis.py`, so the central "learned stopping rule beats heuristics" comparison is confounded by leakage and may reverse under an honest split. On top of that, the reported "corrected boundary" is computed by `first_zero_crossing`, which does **not** implement the stated optimal-stop rule (`inf{t>=2: mu_t<=0}`) and returns step 1 for 4 of 6 shipped models, and the **oracle is not floored at t>=2** while every detector is, baking a structural gap into all comparisons. The data-collection layer is mostly sound (padding/alignment, temporal labels, shuffle determinism, GSM8K `####` parsing all verified correct), but an **orchestrator completeness bug** lets a `--smoke` leftover poison full runs in the cross-family aggregate. None of the conclusions can be trusted until the graders are fixed, labels recomputed, and the analysis is re-run out-of-sample with a t>=2-floored oracle and a corrected boundary operator.

## 3. Data-collection correctness

**We mostly fetch the right data correctly, but two grader defects corrupt the single most important field, and one provenance/orchestration defect can silently substitute the wrong sample.**

The trace mechanics are verified sound. Batched left-padded generation, attention masking, and the teacher-forcing logit/hidden-state slicing have **no off-by-one** — a numerical teacher-forcing reproduction matched to ~2.4e-7, and the `prompt_width-1` (logits) vs `prompt_width` (hidden) offset is intentional and correct (`real_trace_experiments.py:1228-1230, 1382-1474`). There is **no ground-truth leakage into the prompt**: `conversation_prompt` renders only the model's own `thought/answer/confidence/stop`; the gold answer rides on the history row but is never rendered (`631-676`, `1861-1910`). Temporal labels are **correctly constructed**: `groupby("run_id")` (unique per model/task/temp/seed) prevents `shift(-1)` from crossing temperature/seed, the last step is dropped from transitions, and all features are backward-looking (`trace_analysis.py:47-60, 188`). GSM8K `####` parsing, the `max_tasks` select guard, and `shuffle(seed=17)` content-independent positional determinism are all empirically verified correct (`849-853, 868-871`).

But the gold/predicted **grading is the weak point**, and it is the field everything depends on. Three confirmed grader bugs deflate or inflate `correct`: the fraction-first precedence and ordinal-word-as-fraction bugs (both **critical**, GSM8K), and the RHS-only `=` split (**critical**, MATH — accepts `"0"` for an equation). Compounding these are the freeform last-number heuristic firing on weak cues like "so"/"therefore" and grabbing trailing confidence/step/year tokens (**high**: ~36% of step rows on this path, 7,310 confirmed false negatives + 1,391 false positives), plus medium-severity `limit_denominator` digit-count snapping (a non-monotone correctness boundary at ~7 sig-figs) and unit-stripping that equates `30` with `30°`/`50%`. Finally, the orchestrator's `collection_complete` can mark a 6-task smoke leftover as a finished full cell (**critical**), pulling a statistically meaningless trace set into the aggregate. Net: the harness records the right observables correctly, but the label and the sample population can be silently wrong.

## 4. Analysis correctness

**As written, the analysis cannot validly derive or benchmark the detection algorithm — it leaks, mis-defines the boundary, and compares against an unreachable oracle.** Even with perfect labels, the conclusions would not survive scrutiny.

The most serious analysis class is **in-sample evaluation with no holdout anywhere** in `trace_analysis.py` (confirmed by grep: no `train_test_split`/`KFold`/`GroupKFold`/`cross_val`). The q-probe, `hazard_drift`, and the tuned entropy/answer-stability thresholds are all fit and scored on the same `step_frame`, so every reported AUC/Brier and every learned detector's `oracle_gap` is optimistically biased. The rule-based baselines have no learnable parameters and cannot overfit, so the comparison is structurally tilted toward the learned detectors — the headline "hazard/drift stop beats heuristics" is exactly the claim most threatened by an honest leave-task-out re-run. The authors already implement the correct discipline (LOFO + train/test generalization gap + ECE) in `equation_analysis.py`, so the fix is to mirror it, not invent it.

The second class is **theory-fidelity of the boundary**. `first_zero_crossing` is not the stated stopping time: it demands a strict pos→nonpos transition, lacks the t>=2 floor, and falls back to step 1 — which it does for 4 of 6 shipped models (verified against `cross_family_summary.csv`). The detector `hazard_stop_for_group` does it correctly, so the *reported* boundary and the *evaluated* stopping rule are two different operators on the same curve. Worse, the **oracle is unfloored** (`idxmax` over all steps) while detectors are floored at t>=2, so a structural gap ≥0.05 contaminates every comparison and is largest for weak models. The `pooled_proxy_drift` estimator additionally **double-multiplies marginals** (computes `(1-q)²α - q²β - λ`, a quantity with no theoretical meaning), so the "correction" narrative compares against a malformed baseline; `conditional_empirical_gap` is identically ~0 by construction (vacuous if cited); `fitted_hazard_drift` extrapolates each hazard model off its training support. The anytime-valid machinery is also mis-stated: the "mixture e-process" is a per-step batch e-value union bound, not a Ville/martingale process over time (no optional-stopping guarantee as claimed); the union-bound weights use `(t+1)²` and spend only ~0.0196 of the nominal 0.05 (conservative, not invalid); EB uses `ddof=1` vs the proof's `ddof=0`; and EB is structurally infeasible until n≈1000+, so on the n=900 cross-family runs it degenerates to `never_stop` (last-step fallback, ~100% false-late) yet is ranked as a detector. Finally, `capability_gate`'s OR-branch lets ~3% step-1-accuracy models pass the accuracy floor (uncertain finding — the cross-family conclusion code does not actually emit the >=2-family claim, but the near-chance-q_1 witnesses warrant a confound caveat). Until the boundary operator, the oracle floor, the leakage, and the pooled-drift estimator are fixed, the detection algorithm's derivation and its claimed superiority are not established.
