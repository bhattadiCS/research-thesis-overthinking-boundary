What does “less generated text” count?
Both paths share their generated responses until the early path stops. After that, only the five-response path continues.
This comparison still generates all five responses once. The difference shows the extra text the early path would avoid in a standalone run. Matching answer text does not establish correctness.

      

      
A little more about this demo+
What is a response?
One complete answer or revision. A token is a small piece of generated text, so fewer tokens generally means less text was produced.
Why stop with low estimates?
The learned rule compares the estimated change with a continuation penalty. Low estimates do not force it to keep trying: the next response may look only slightly more promising.
What is not being checked?
The app has no reference answer for your question. The stopping score can be wrong, an early stop can miss a later correction, and extra responses can also make an answer worse.
